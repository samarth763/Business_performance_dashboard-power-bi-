

💻 Business Performance Analysis (2021–2022)
📊 Project Overview

I created this project to analyze business performance for the period 2021–2022. The dashboard focuses on sales, cost, orders, quantity sold, profit, product categories, brands, and state-wise performance.

The dashboard was designed to give a quick overview of the business and make it easier to compare product and brand performance.

🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

Analyze overall revenue and profit
Compare total cost with revenue
Analyze quantity sold across product categories
Compare sales performance by brand
Analyze profit across different states
Understand total orders and quantity
View supervisor information
Identify categories with higher and lower sales quantities

🔍 Key Insights
1. Category Performance

Monitors had the highest quantity sold with 1,843 units, followed by Mouse with 1,496 units and CPU with 1,450 units.

2. Lower-Selling Categories

Printer, NIC, Computer Case, and Motherboard were among the categories with comparatively lower quantities sold.

3. Brand-wise Sales

Intel had the highest share of sales at approximately 19%, followed by Samsung at 16% and Dell at 14%.

4. Overall Profitability

The business generated approximately 99M in revenue against 76M in total cost, resulting in approximately 23M total profit.

5. State-wise Performance

The map visualization shows profit distribution across different states in India, helping identify geographical differences in business performance.

💡 Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

Focus on high-performing categories such as Monitors, Mouse, and CPU.
Analyze demand for lower-performing categories such as Printers and NICs.
Continue monitoring the performance of major brands such as Intel, Samsung, and Dell.
Use state-wise profit data to identify locations with stronger business performance.
Analyze low-performing categories to determine whether pricing, promotion, or inventory changes could improve sales.
